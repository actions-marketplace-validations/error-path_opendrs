## Decision (spec §10.1, applied exactly)

- Lift 1.931× ≥ 1.2× → the fact ships with the tuned defaults.
- AUC-gain 95 % CI -0.0003 to -0.0001 excludes 0, but negative (adding the signal measurably, if very slightly, hurts AUC — not the positive gain the rule asks for) → it stays brief-only.

## Caveats

- **No rename detection.** The per-commit file lists come from `git log --name-only --no-renames` (not the shipped detector's `-M`), so a rename shows as a delete and an add rather than one path. `-M` is what made the first attempt at this time out on the larger histories.
- **`exists at head` is not checked.** The shipped detector drops a fact when the partner was later deleted; this backtest has no notion of "head" beyond the commit itself, so it always treats a historically co-changing partner as present.
- **The grid is chosen by train lift alone**, with no minimum fire count; a combination that rarely fires can still win on a lucky train split. The chosen point here fires on 3415 of 41969 train commits, which is not a small sample.
